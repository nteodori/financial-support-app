# Meridian Pay - Commercial Card Overview

Meridian Pay issues corporate Visa and Mastercard payment cards designed for small businesses, mid-market companies, and startups.

## Card Types Offered
1. **Physical Corporate Cards**: Issued to employees for T&E expenses. Requires verified business address and KYC-verified cardholder.
2. **Virtual Single-Use Cards**: Designed for one-time vendor payments or software subscriptions with automated expiration and exact dollar authorizations.
3. **Virtual Multi-Use Cards**: Reusable cards with departmental spending limits and automated receipt matching.

## Digital Wallet Provisioning
Both physical and virtual cards can be added to Apple Pay and Google Wallet instantly upon card creation, even before the physical card arrives.

## Default Spending Limits
- Standard Card Limit: Up to the account credit limit, customizable per cardholder down to $100/day.
- Daily Cash Advance Limit: $500 per card (requires explicit company administrator permission).
