# Credit Limits and Adjustment Guidelines (Product Overview)

Meridian Pay provides revolving credit lines and charge limits based on business account tiers:

## Account Tier Baseline Limits
- **Starter Tier**: $2,500 – $10,000 credit limit.
- **Growth Tier**: $10,000 – $50,000 credit limit.
- **Enterprise Tier**: $50,000 – $500,000+ custom credit facility.

## Standard In-App Limit Increase Guidelines (Legacy Reference)
As stated in customer-facing help center materials:
"Accounts in good standing for over 60 days may request a routine automated limit increase of up to 10% directly through their support representative or account dashboard without formal underwriting resubmission."

*Important Note*: Always verify against the current Underwriting and Risk Policy (`policy/limit-increase-policy.md`) prior to executing any manual credit adjustment, as risk conditions supersede routine guidelines.
