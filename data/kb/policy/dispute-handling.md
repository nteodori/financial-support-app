# Policy: Dispute Handling and Provisional Credit (General Overview)

Meridian Pay complies with Regulation E and card network dispute rules for commercial accounts.

## General Dispute Eligibility
- Cardholders may dispute charges billed incorrectly, duplicate charges, recurring subscriptions canceled prior to billing, or unrecognized merchant authorizations.
- Disputes must be initiated within 60 days of the statement date.
- Cardholders are encouraged to contact the merchant first for friendly refund resolution if the merchant is recognized.

## General Provisional Credit Rule (Legacy Section 4.2)
"For routine unrecognized transaction disputes under $250, support agents may open the formal network dispute and issue an immediate provisional credit directly to the customer ledger while the investigation is pending."

*ATTENTION*: This general provision is strictly governed by the Fraud Escalation Thresholds (`policy/fraud-thresholds.md`). Agents must review account age and risk profile before applying provisional credits or filing disputes on high-stakes transactions.
